Naming system: 

Prefix:
 - "Lava" for the type of tile
Affix Types and order:
 - Thickness level: (1 to 4) i.e. "1" to "4"
 - Glazed sides, how many they are and the location of those sides: "G2" "TL" meanig "G2"= Glazed sides 2 and "TL"= Top and Left
 - Corners Painted, how many they are and the location of those painted corners: "C3" "TLTRBR" meaning "C3" = 3 Corners painted and the locations "TLTRBR" = Top Left, Top Right, Bottom Right
-Affixes are written Clock-wise i.e. 
 - "Top->Right->Bottom->Left" for glazed sides
 - "Top Right->Bottom Right->Bottom Left->TopLeft" for corners
Example:
-Lava->(1-4)->G(1-4(location))->C(1-4(location))
 - Type of tile followed by thickness level followed by Glazed sides number and their location followed by painted Corners number and their location

Affixes will be input only if they exist on the tile, if not, their abbreviations will not be typed in.


Examples: 


-Lava1G2BLC1BR
 -Lava, Thickness1, Glazed sides:2 - Bottom and Left, Corner painted:1 - Bottom-Right

-Lava2G1LC2TRBL
 -Lava, Thickness2, Glazed sides:1 - Left, Corners painted:2 - Top-Right and Bottom-left

-Lava3G3TRB
 -Lava, Thickness3, Glazed sides:3 - Top, Right, Bottom (No painted corners)

-Lava4G2RL 
 -Lava, Thickness4, Glazed sides:4 - Right and Left (No painted corners)

-Lava2C3TLBRTR
 - Lava, Thickness 2, Corners painted:3 - Top-Left, Bottom-Right, Top-Right (No Glazed Sides)
